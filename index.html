Remap your mouse side-buttons.

Flexi Input is a minimal menu bar app for macOS that turns the extra buttons on your mouse into keyboard shortcuts — configured per device, no driver bloat.

Download for macOS See features

Version 1.3.3 · released 2026-05-09 · macOS 14 Sonoma or later · Universal (Apple Silicon + Intel)

How it works

1. Detect

Move the cursor into the detection area and click a side-button. Flexi Input shows the button number and which mouse it came from.

2. Bind

Pick a hotkey — any combination with Cmd, Ctrl, Option, or Shift. Save, and the binding takes effect immediately.

3. Use

Pressing that side-button now fires the hotkey instead of its original click. Bindings are remembered per device.

Why Flexi Input

  • Per-device bindings — your travel mouse and desk mouse can have different layouts.
  • Natural scroll toggle that works for every mouse, even ones macOS forgets.
  • Notarized by Apple. Hardened runtime. No background daemons.
  • Open infrastructure: signed appcast, automatic updates planned, no telemetry.
